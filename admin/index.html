SharePoint mirror queue

Rows pending/failed = not yet mirrored to SharePoint. The mirror worker retries failed rows and the database re-queues them every 15 minutes, up to 20 attempts each.

Recycle bin

Deleted assets live here. Restore puts them back on the register; purge removes them permanently (mirrored as deleted to SharePoint).
